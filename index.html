Lash care

Keep them full between visits.

  • First 24 hrs

    Keep them drySkip steam, saunas and long hot showers while the adhesive cures.

  • Daily

    Brush with a spoolieA gentle brush-through every morning keeps the fan shape crisp.

  • A few times a week

    Give them a lash bathOil-free foam cleanser, rinse and pat dry. Clean lashes last longer.

  • 2–3 weeks

    Book your fillNatural lashes shed every day. A fill brings the set back to full.
